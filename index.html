Ask for light

Before you look at your sins, look at God. Ask the Holy Spirit to show you what you need to see, honestly and without fear.

Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of thy faithful and kindle in them the fire of thy love. Enlighten my mind to know my sins, move my heart to sorrow for them, and give me the grace to confess them humbly and to amend my life. Amen.
What to bring. The Church asks that every mortal sin be confessed by its kind and, as nearly as you can, how often. A sin is mortal when it involves grave matter and is committed with full knowledge and deliberate consent (CCC 1857). Confessing venial sins is also strongly encouraged. Aim for honesty, not anxiety; if you are unsure about something, simply bring it and ask the priest.

In the confessional

Confess

You can kneel behind the screen or sit face to face, whichever you prefer. The priest will guide you if you lose your place.

  1. Greeting

    The priest welcomes you. Make the Sign of the Cross.

    In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

  2. Begin

    Bless me, Father, for I have sinned.

    The priest may invite you to trust in God's mercy, or read a short passage of Scripture.

  3. Confess your sins

    Name them simply and directly: mortal sins by kind and number as best you can, then any venial sins. When you've finished:

    For these and all the sins of my past life, I am sorry.

  4. Counsel and penance

    The priest may offer advice and will give you a penance: a prayer or an act. If you don't understand it, ask.

  5. Act of Contrition

    When invited, pray aloud:

    O my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended Thee, and I detest all my sins because of Thy just punishments, but most of all because they offend Thee, my God, who art all good and deserving of all my love. I firmly resolve, with the help of Thy grace, to sin no more and to avoid the near occasion of sin. Amen.

    If the words won't come, the tax collector's prayer is enough:

    O God, be merciful to me a sinner. Luke 18:13

  6. Absolution

    The priest extends his hand over you and prays the words of absolution. Make the Sign of the Cross as he names the Trinity, and answer:

    Amen.

  7. Dismissal

    The priest may say, “Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.”

    His mercy endures for ever.

    Thank him, and go in peace.

Go in peace

Afterward

“Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered.”Psalm 31:1 (32:1), Douay-Rheims

Do it soon, ideally before you leave the church.

Give thanks

Lord Jesus, you have met me here and sent me away clean. Thank you for your mercy, which is greater than all my sin. Let me not return to what you have forgiven; keep me close to you, and make me merciful to others as you have been merciful to me. Amen.
Remember the mercy. You can record this confession in your journal: what you brought, your penance, and the grace you received. Or you can clear it and let it go entirely. Either is a good choice.

    About

    Return

    A companion for the Sacrament of Reconciliation: to prepare well, confess with confidence, and remember the mercy received.

    How it works

    1. Prepare: pray for light and note how long it's been since your last confession.
    2. Examine: reflect on the Ten Commandments and the Precepts of the Church.
    3. Review: see what you've marked, gathered in one place.
    4. Confess: follow the rite step by step, with the words to say.
    5. Afterward: give thanks, then record the confession or clear it.

    The Journal keeps the confessions you choose to record: what you brought, your penance, and the grace you received.

    Privacy

    Your examination is held only while the app is open and is kept only if you record it. The journal is stored on this device alone and is never sent anywhere. You can protect it with a passcode, which encrypts it, and delete any entry, or everything, at any time.

    Offline

    After the first visit, Return works without a connection. For the most reliable storage, add it to your Home Screen.

    Sources

    Scripture is from the Douay-Rheims Bible and the Act of Contrition is the traditional form, both in the public domain. The examination questions and the prayer of thanksgiving are original. The words of absolution are described rather than quoted.

    Return is an aid to preparation, not a substitute for the guidance of a priest. When in doubt about anything, bring it to confession and ask.
